How SmartServe Works

End-to-end civic complaint lifecycle from submission to resolution.

1
Submit Form

Citizen provides complaint title, selects civic category, and provides detailed description.

2
Auto ID Generation

System auto-generates a collision-free Tracking ID and timestamps the request in MongoDB.

3
Admin Assessment

Authorized officer reviews complaint, marks status as IN_PROGRESS, and adds internal remarks.

4
Resolution & Closure

Ground team resolves the grievance. Status is marked RESOLVED, visible instantly to citizen.
